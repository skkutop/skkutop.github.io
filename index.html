About

Mira is a fictional creative technologist exploring gameful interaction, generative visuals, and gentle software rituals. She treats interfaces as places to pause, notice, and try a different route.

Her imaginary studio practice pairs a systems-minded approach with a love of tiny details: a satisfying transition, an understandable empty state, or a control that invites experimentation instead of anxiety.

04

Research notes

Mira’s fictional research asks how digital products can be clear without becoming cold. Recent imaginary notes look at feedback that teaches through play, motion that confirms rather than distracts, and systems that leave room for personality.

Each note begins as a quick question, becomes a small interactive sketch, and only grows into a larger system when the sketch has something useful to say.
